# Urgent Care Clinical Policy: CTAS Escalation & Shift Handoff
Document ID: POL-UC-2024-08
Effective Date: March 2024
Owner: Clinical Quality & Safety Committee

## 1. Triage Re-evaluation Standards
1.1 Patients designated Canadian Triage and Acuity Scale (CTAS) Level 3 must be re-evaluated every 30 minutes if waiting in the waiting room during surge conditions.
1.2 CTAS Level 2 patients must be assigned to an immediate care space upon availability, with vital signs recorded every 15 minutes.

## 2. Shift Handoff Requirements
2.1 Incoming and outgoing Charge Nurses must conduct a joint walk-through of the waiting room at every shift transition (07:00, 15:00, 23:00).
2.2 High-risk patients (CTAS 2/3 exceeding waiting time benchmarks by >45 minutes) must be explicitly flagged in the electronic handoff log, signed off by both outgoing and incoming Charge Nurses.
