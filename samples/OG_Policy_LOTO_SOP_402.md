# Standard Operating Procedure: SAGD Steam Manifold Isolation & LOTO
Document ID: SOP-OPS-SAGD-402
Effective Date: January 2025
Owner: Asset Integrity & HSE Operations

## 1. Shift Handover & Isolation Protocol
1.1 Any active isolation on high-pressure steam lines (>2000 kPa) undergoing maintenance must be physically inspected by both the off-going Field Operator and on-coming Lead Operator at shift change.
1.2 The Lock-Out/Tag-Out (LOTO) master log sheet must be signed in-person at the central control room prior to issuing work permits for the incoming shift.

## 2. Zero Energy State Verification
2.1 Pressure bleed-off verification must be documented with a dual-signature (Operator and HSE Specialist) on Form LOTO-B before removing secondary containment barriers.
